About The Studio

Precision. Proportion. Presence.

The Golden Ratio Salon was built on a simple belief: real beauty work isn't about following trends — it's about understanding the proportions, tones and textures unique to you, then refining them with technical precision.

Our stylists, colourists and makeup artists train continuously on international technique while staying rooted in what actually suits Indian skin, hair texture and climate. From a quick blow-dry before a meeting to full bridal transformation, every appointment is run with the same studio discipline.

Located at Bengali Square, behind Medinova Retreat, the studio is designed as a calm, private space — no rush, no crowding, just focused work.
